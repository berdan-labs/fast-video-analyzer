# Code of Conduct

Fast Video Analyzer is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically
constructive community. Participants must act professionally, respect different
backgrounds and viewpoints, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather
than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
deliberate disruption, doxxing, sharing private information without consent,
and publishing credentials, private media, model weights, or other sensitive
material.

Report community conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the
contact listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). For security
vulnerabilities, use the private security advisory process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled confidentially where
possible. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other action necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
